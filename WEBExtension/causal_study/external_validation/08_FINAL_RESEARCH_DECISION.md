# Final research decision

## What survives independent verification?

A separately implemented reanalysis reproduces the historical matched-readout observation on the same 300 questions: partitioned Broadcast–NoComm changes from +8 pp original plurality to +1 pp aware plurality, with a −7 pp interaction; lexical-aware yields 0. The arithmetic attenuation is 87.5%. With a prespecified conservative strict-majority rule, NoComm 9/300 and Broadcast 32/300 retain +7.667 pp. Therefore communication's measured benefit depends materially on the decision contract, and ignoring abstentions is not a universally correct repair. The synthetic unsupported-question counterexample establishes a logical safety boundary, not an empirical unsupported error rate.

## Does it extend beyond this implementation?

**The specific abstention mechanism has not been independently replicated.** Eight external model configurations on 500 shared GSM8K questions reconstruct exactly under their source numeric contract. Original and aware readouts coincide; alternative tie/consensus readouts produce different communication comparisons. This independently illustrates generic readout sensitivity, already familiar in the literature, but supplies neither explicit abstention labels nor unsupported Web-QA outcomes. It cannot establish the source-sufficiency interaction or a population null for it.

## Is there a meaningful Web-specific contribution, and what is new?

None is established yet. The present Web component is static Wikipedia-derived evidence. MuSiQue-Full is suitable for a source-bound check: 4834 labeled records form 2417 same-question ID pairs but 2410 distinct normalized question-text units, with source documents and an existing sufficiency contract. Its author definitions/license/evaluator were checked; the acquired mirror matches its published hash, but author-archive byte equality remains unverified. Two independent blinded label packages are prepared and PENDING. New inference/semantic validation results are absent.

Proposal/readout separation, collaboration-assisted abstention, context-sufficiency calibration and aggregator/debate ablations all have close precedents. A potentially distinct finding would require an independently consequential peer-versus-private readout interaction that depends on source sufficiency and survives competent controls. The intersection alone is not novelty. Packaging these scripts does not create a benchmark contribution.

## Can the claim be tested with a feasible budget?

The arithmetic is explicit, but conservative feasibility is unresolved. The conditional 20-question/two-backbone pilot costs 720 successful completions. The locked 1000-question confirmation costs 36000, excluding the pilot and with separate failure reserves. A 5 pp interaction needs approximately 951 questions at assumed SD 0.5 and 80% normal sensitivity, but 3803 at SD 1 and 15209 at the universal SD upper value 2; both exceed the 2366-question disjoint confirmation pool. Historical token references are lower bounds where usage is missing. Actual deployment prices, immutable serving identity, tokenization and long-context latency remain unresolved. No powered or dollar-feasible promise is made.
