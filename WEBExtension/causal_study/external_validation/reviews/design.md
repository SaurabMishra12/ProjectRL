## Judgment

**REVISE before any paid pilot.** The proposed question-clustered comparison can identify a narrowly defined readout-by-peer-packet interaction. It cannot yet establish a general failure of multi-agent Web QA. A source-grounded task contract, independent sufficiency review and an executable freeze are prerequisites for spending. A conditional plan with unknown data access, exact model revisions or parsing rules must remain explicitly blocked.

The important unresolved question is whether an apparent communication gain changes when the same competent readout is applied to private and communicating agents, and whether that change reverses when abstention is the appropriate system behavior. This matters beyond ProjectRL in principle. Its natural prevalence and external transportability remain empirical questions; neither a logical counterexample nor another reanalysis of the original 300 identities settles them.

## What the architecture identifies

Three shared initial responses followed by three private revisions and three peer revisions per context give a useful control for the number of requested update calls. Applying every deterministic readout offline to each frozen final state separates readout sensitivity from fresh generation noise. Pairing the sufficient and insufficient contexts within the unique question, and resampling whole questions, respects the benchmark's dependence structure.

The proposed custom outcome is supported-answer EM on sufficient contexts and a valid explicit abstention on insufficient contexts. For context `c`, the interaction is `(U(peer, aware, c) - U(private, aware, c)) - (U(peer, original, c) - U(private, original, c))`. Averaging its two contexts is mathematically coherent, but identifies a policy-weighted utility interaction under a deliberate 50/50 context mix. It is not automatically a deployment average or an official MuSiQue metric.

Equal update-call counts do not match input/output tokens, latency or information content. A peer packet can bundle candidate answers, citations and source excerpts. Its measured effect is the effect of that fixed packet and protocol, not an isolated effect of topology, evidence verification, explanation quality or citation faithfulness. The precise packet, source partition rule, fixed seat order, roles and revision prompts must be frozen and reported. Randomizing private/peer execution order addresses temporal scheduling; it does not remove every provider-side dependence or make different questions the same treatment unit.

## Material issues requiring resolution

1. **The primary average can hide false answers.** An improvement on sufficient contexts can exactly cancel harm on insufficient contexts. The existing interaction unit test intentionally exhibits such cancellation. Require both context-specific interaction estimates, unsupported false-answer rates and their paired changes, and answer accuracy/coverage/selective risk alongside the primary average. A positive average never licenses a claim that abstention-aware voting is safe. A minimum meaningful interaction and any tolerated false-answer increase must be chosen before outcomes, with the latter justified by the intended source-grounded task rather than selected for significance.

2. **Source sufficiency is a task definition, not world knowledge.** MuSiQue's answerability field concerns the supplied context. A question can have an answer in the world while its context lacks the necessary chain. Explicitly instruct models to answer only from the supplied evidence. Preserve the benchmark label, source bank and aliases separately; do not infer insufficiency from missing supporting-fact IDs alone. Blinded reviewers should inspect the question and full context union without arm labels, proposals or correctness changes, independently label sufficiency/ambiguity, and adjudicate disagreements. All unresolved labels remain pending. Do not remove difficult cases after observing model behavior; retain the frozen main population and provide a label-ambiguity sensitivity analysis.

3. **Official MuSiQue prediction fields are separate.** The saved original evaluator consumes `predicted_answer` and `predicted_answerable`, scores answer EM/F1 only on answerable rows, and also computes grouped answer/sufficiency metrics across paired rows. A Hotpot-style plurality comparator is not the original MuSiQue aggregator. Call it a ProjectRL plurality comparator. Preserve raw answer strings and the explicit sufficiency flag, and report official metrics separately from the new custom utility. The present contracts module has Hotpot and GSM adapters, not a completed MuSiQue adapter; do not call its behavior a frozen MuSiQue implementation until the adapter and official-evaluator agreement are tested.

4. **Readout fallback can collapse technically distinct categories.** The typed proposals correctly distinguish substantive answers, expressed abstentions, provider refusals, empty outputs, missing outputs and malformed outputs. However, the current aware fallback converts an all-refusal pool to `Proposal('noanswer','abstain')`; a strict-majority rejection can also become an aggregate abstention after technical failures. This can be an intentional system-level fallback, but it must not be reported as model-expressed abstention or silently earn the same diagnostic label. Preserve a decision reason and report proposal-level refusals/failures as well as final decisions. A provider refusal is not an independent judgment that the evidence is insufficient. Predefine whether system fallback abstentions earn custom utility and show a sensitivity analysis if they do.

5. **Failures cannot select the analyzed cohort.** Define parse failure, refusal, timeout, indeterminate execution and retries prospectively. Preserve the original three seats, all attempts and all question identities. An unavailable seat must not silently reduce the strict-majority denominator. Primary failure handling must be identical across arms, with a complete-case sensitivity only as secondary. Retry ceilings need explicit authorization before execution; unused historical E6 completions supply none.

6. **External traces need the right treatment structure.** Independent initial-proposal traces can test a frozen-proposal readout interaction with abstention semantics only if those semantics are actually present. They cannot estimate a communication-by-readout interaction without corresponding communicating and private states. Missing numeric extraction in a GSM parser is not expressed abstention. A trace collection with no relevant abstentions supplies little evidence about this failure mode, even if every file is reproducible. Report compatibility limitations rather than treating unavailable semantics as a null result.

7. **Freshness and heterogeneity need prospective rules.** Fresh MuSiQue identities should be selected before any generation using a published hash/order rule; no correctness, disagreement or initial abstention filter belongs in primary inclusion. Cross-check overlap with previously exposed question strings. This means fresh to this research procedure, not proven absent from model pretraining. Choose a second model by an outcome-independent rule and identify exact deployments/revisions. Model and context contrasts share questions; they are not additional independent question samples. Report model-specific effects and prespecified heterogeneity rather than choosing a favorable backbone or pooling opposite effects into a persuasive mean.

8. **Power must address this outcome.** The four-cell question interaction, averaged over paired contexts, is not a binary McNemar outcome. Do not substitute old Broadcast-versus-NoComm discordance or the eight-question E6 variance. State the support and variance assumptions of the exact clustered outcome, include conservative variance sensitivity, and separate an operational pilot from a powered confirmation. Unknown model/context token lengths require budget sensitivity; equal calls do not imply equal total cost. Repeated offline readouts cost no inference and add no independent observations.

## All five outcomes remain reportable

| Observed result | Defensible conclusion | Claim that would fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Broadcast still helps under matched aggregation | Communication has incremental value under the tested packet, model and context mix; readout sensitivity may coexist with it. Report both the matched effect and interaction. | The original gain was wholly an aggregation artifact, or communication generally has no value. |
| Communication offers little incremental value | Estimate a bounded, model- and task-specific effect with its interval. A null result is useful if the design is powered for the prespecified meaningful effect. | A nonsignificant estimate proves equivalence, or multi-agent communication is universally unnecessary. |
| Abstention-aware voting helps answerable QA but harms insufficient QA | The evaluation contract creates a real accuracy/rejection tradeoff. Report both components and false answers; a positive balanced utility cannot erase harm. | Ignoring abstentions is a uniformly safe aggregator improvement. |
| Models or datasets have opposite effects | The phenomenon is heterogeneous; identify the prespecified conditions and uncertainty, and preserve all results. | Selecting the favorable model establishes a general direction, or pooled cancellation establishes no problem. |
| The issue disappears under a standard competent aggregator | The demonstrated weakness may be a limited implementation audit. A broader findings claim needs independent prevalence and material residual consequences. | Renaming an ordinary plurality correction constitutes a new general theory or automatically justifies a WWW main-track contribution. |

## Recommendation to the lead investigator

Retain a zero-call decision while the external availability and source-sufficiency gates are unresolved. A small operational pilot becomes scientifically defensible only after the actual data, source-grounded contract, prompts, model revisions, failure policy and reporting components are frozen. Even then it is a feasibility check, not external confirmation by itself. Favor a limited implementation-audit conclusion if the effect disappears under a competent baseline and external evidence does not establish prevalence.

**Material issues at the inspected snapshot, in order of consequence:**

1. The written prospective failure convention is not yet enforced by a prospective-arm scoring function. Report 05 says that incomplete required arm outputs receive utility zero under every readout. Calling the current bare readouts on `[answer('Paris'), answer('Paris'), missing]` returns `Paris` under all four rules. That is valid behavior for a general readout helper, but insufficient to implement the prospective ITT contract. Before launch, add and test an arm-level validity/utility boundary which implements the declared policy, preserves all three seats and reports missing-cell bounds; do not silently apply the bare helper as the prospective scorer. Invalid initial states also need an explicit rule because the peer packet requires parsed initial fields. No prospective scientific result currently depends on this mismatch because none has been generated.

2. The updated power source correctly simulates only a realizable `J ∈ {-1,0,+1}` sensitivity law and rejects second moment above one. However, the generated JSON and report 06 inspected in this pass still contain an SD2 Monte Carlo row (`support=[-2,0,2]`, reported power `0.80135`). They are stale relative to the corrected source. Regenerate all power artifacts and reports, then refresh freeze hashes before final delivery. SD2 may remain an explicitly loose normal-theory bound; it must not be presented as a simulated proposal-realizable law. The normal sample sizes themselves remain a sensitivity calculation, not a distribution-free power guarantee.

3. The new MuSiQue adapter still needs strict provider-envelope validation before execution. A direct probe with `provider_refusal='true'` plus an otherwise valid answer is accepted as an answer; `provider_refusal=True` plus substantive answer fields is classified as refusal without identifying the contradiction. Either reject non-Boolean/contradictory envelopes upstream with a tested invariant or define the precedence explicitly. Do not let provider metadata corruption masquerade as a semantic result. The intentionally documented rule that `predicted_answerable=False` overrides retained answer text is coherent and is not this issue; preserve the raw fields for official scoring and auditing.

**Revisions that satisfactorily address the earlier design objections:**

- All three agents now receive the same full official context; the reports explicitly state that this is transport to a source-bound reading-comprehension regime rather than a literal replication of partitioned evidence. The exact peer fields, fixed seats and source ordering are specified. Claims about partitioned Web evidence or evidence acquisition therefore remain outside its estimand.
- The average custom-utility interaction has mandatory context-specific components, unsupported false-answer changes and coverage/selective risk. The prohibition on a safety claim from the average is appropriate. Official MuSiQue answer and grouped sufficiency metrics remain separately named.
- The MuSiQue adapter now distinguishes explicit Boolean answerability from world-reference answer text and rejects true-flag `noanswer`, empty true-flag answers and invalid source indices. An all-refusal aware readout now remains refusal and earns no valid-abstention credit. This resolves the concrete all-refusal collapse in the initial review; conservative quorum fallback remains a declared system action whose provenance must be reported.
- Both contexts remain in one unique-question cluster. The two backbone tests use Bonferroni alpha 0.025 each, and the frozen absolute meaningful interaction is 0.05. A studentized mean test addresses the four-cell contrast; McNemar is correctly excluded. The feasible sensitivity law's +1 construction is consistent with the frozen readout states, and the 0/-1 cases are obtainable by equality/swapping. One selected sparse law per variance is a sensitivity example, not a guarantee over every distribution with that variance.
- The arithmetic architecture is 18 calls per question/backbone, 36 across both backbones: 720 successful completions for 20 pilot identities and 36,000 for 1,000 confirmation identities. Pilot and confirmation are disjoint. The 1,000-question choice is explicitly conditional on SD0.5-like variance, while SD1's normal sensitivity exceeds the 2,373-question available confirmation pool. The 10% reserves are hard attempt allowances, not promises that completion will be achieved. These are counts, not approved spending.
- Model artifact revisions, temperature 0.7 and distinct initial cache namespaces avoid claiming that three greedy identical prompts create diverse agents. Serving runtime, actual deployment equivalence, tokenization, source authentication, annotation completion, prices and human authorization are explicitly unresolved launch gates. A conditional specification with these gates is a defensible deliverable; it is not an executable freeze.

It does not yet justify a paid pilot or establish a general Web-specific failure mode. The five outcome-dependent conclusions in the initial review still apply unchanged.
