# Phase C: Novelty gap and related work

Date checked: 15 October 2026; bounded search refreshed 16 October 2026. Scientific status: **REVISE; novelty remains conditional**. No paid inference was used.

## Finding

The broad proposal-utilization account is not a new scientific contribution. The nearest work already separates candidate availability, revision and final readout, including fixed-proposal evidence probes. Likewise, retrieving evidence, distributing it across agents, synthesizing candidates, and using asymmetric evidence access have direct precedents. Moving those observations to HotpotQA or adding a SparseComm implementation would not establish novelty.

A narrower diagnostic remains worth specifying: **When candidate answers and retrieved source contents are fixed, does preserving the relation between a claim and the source that actually supports it change collective answer selection?** This is a candidate gap, not a verified absence in the literature. Source attribution and citation-faithfulness work, including the September 2026 citation-laundering study R11, creates substantial overlap. A binding-corruption result could otherwise amount to a familiar attribution vulnerability in another pipeline.

## Three candidate contrasts and their novelty risks

1. **Frozen proposals plus source access.** Keep the candidate set and finalizer fixed; compare a prespecified retrieved packet with a length-matched irrelevant packet. This targets the effect of informative source contents on selection. It has the clearest connection to hypothesis B, but a positive result largely extends established verification-aware readout and RAG. A useful secondary contrast, weak as a standalone WWW story. The source packet must be selected without the answer key; matched tokens do not equal matched informativeness.

2. **Evidence accessibility under independently qualified joins.** Cross evidence ownership with fixed recipient-update opportunities on questions whose intended answer requires an independently annotated cross-source join. Preserve the global union, local quotas and model settings. This targets an access bottleneck, but R6 already preserves a global pool and changes its distribution. The possible advance is a precisely identified necessary-access interaction under documented source sufficiency, not a first discovery of information asymmetry. Annotation uncertainty and feasible fresh cohort size are material barriers. E6’s annotated-article placement alone does not establish this construct.

3. **Source-to-claim binding with frozen content.** Preserve questions, candidate multiplicity/order schedule, source texts/IDs, finalizer calls and requested outputs; swap two existing citation targets across different candidate slots while preserving every candidate and source citation degree. The contrast is the existing mapping versus a degree-preserving swap, not verified-support versus unsupported evidence: existing citations have not all been adjudicated for entailment. This targets reliance on externally checkable support relations rather than the mere presence of evidence. It is experimentally controllable but has substantial novelty risk from citation-faithfulness and citation-laundering work. A reversible swap is an injected defect, not evidence that such defects naturally caused the historical communication effect. The experiment must distinguish answer selection from citation repair, include a plain-source baseline if necessary to interpret metadata reliance, and state exactly whether it changes displayed claim support, source identity, or both. A source-ID renaming that preserves every relation is only an invariance control.

The proposed four-arm answer/explanation/source/both design is **not automatically selected**. It changes multiple things at once: semantic content, token length, available direct-answer information, and possibly the finalizer’s task. Without an explicit estimand and adequate controls, it cannot distinguish source verification from a generic helpful-context effect. More arms do not solve an ambiguous treatment.

## What would count as a Web contribution

A defensible contribution would isolate a failure of evidence transmission or provenance during synthesis from multiple independently retrieved sources, demonstrate its consequence for decisions and auditability, and show when a concrete preservation rule helps or fails. The claim should transfer to retrieval and communication systems with multiple sources; it should not depend on a particular PPO policy or on labeling Wikipedia passages “Web.” Static retrieved Wikipedia contexts support a controlled Web-derived QA claim, not a claim about live search, source freshness, publisher credibility, or hostile open-Web conditions.

Source accessibility, semantic entailment and causal use are distinct. An ID that resolves to a real sentence establishes provenance; a sentence that supports a claim establishes support; neither alone proves the finalizer relied on it. Correct answer strings do not establish correct reasoning. A counterfactual metadata effect establishes sensitivity to that metadata intervention, not an internal reasoning mechanism.

The closest attribution papers now inspected, R10–R12, make the narrow proposal an exploratory diagnostic with uncertain significance. A null effect could mean the model ignores citation links or independently recovers support from the unchanged source bank; it cannot show that source grounding never matters. A positive effect shows sensitivity to the assigned linkage, not automatically reliable verification. **No reviewed contrast yet warrants an unqualified “genuinely novel” claim.** If close attribution work already identifies the same relation under comparable controls, the honest outcome is to stop that narrative or redesign the estimand. Existing descriptive E8 observations remain useful replication/audit evidence regardless.

## Project-specific implications of the offline audit

The Phase A reaggregation changes the practical priority. A fixed, gold-free rule excluding normalized `noanswer` and empty explicit provider abstentions before applying the original majority/tie rule produces **62/300** partitioned initial exact matches, compared with 10 for the original majority and 34 after Broadcast. Against this zero-call baseline Broadcast gains 5 and loses 33 questions. This is a post hoc exploratory reaggregation, not a new confirmatory result or a prior-work finding. It removes the case for spending inference merely to show that initial candidates can be used better. See [Phase A reconstruction](/home/saurab/Documents/projectRLMAIN/WEBExtension/causal_study/next_phase/01_E8_DEEP_TRANSITIONS.md), [diagnostic decisions and paired results](/home/saurab/Documents/projectRLMAIN/WEBExtension/causal_study/next_phase/transition_artifacts/abstention_reaggregation.json), and [fixed rule source](/home/saurab/Documents/projectRLMAIN/WEBExtension/causal_study/next_phase/deep_transitions.py).

Only 20/300 partitioned and 33/300 shared questions have at least two distinct non-abstaining initial answers. Historical disagreement dominated by abstentions is not the conflicting-substantive-answer population a selector study needs. A cache-only study of those cases is a small selected diagnostic; a fresh study must pay for proposal screening and cannot budget only its finalizer arms. No pooled sum of shared/partitioned cells creates additional independent questions. These constraints reinforce **REVISE DESIGN**, with no expenditure justified by this novelty review.

## Recent leads and provenance comparison

R10–R12 have now been compared using the root agent's primary full-text inspection and are included in the matrix. They rule out a first citation intervention, first causal provenance test, generic citation reliability claim, or a novelty claim based only on unchanged source contents with swapped metadata. R12 already changes authorship labels while keeping documents fixed. The frozen-text, degree-preserving candidate–source edge reassignment is a narrower operational distinction; no strong novelty or WWW significance claim follows from that difference alone.

MARCH (ACL 2026), Remember and Reweight (arXiv 2609.03619, 3 September 2026), and A Layered Analysis of Disagreement And Answer Quality (arXiv 2609.08016, 7 September 2026) remain primary discovery leads rather than full-intervention evidence. Search discovery does not imply these are the only recent papers.

The study must retain **REVISE** if its final distinctness argument rests only on “multi-agent,” “WebQA,” “fixed proposals,” “same documents,” “citations,” or “causal”: each term has substantial prior art, and combining labels does not establish a scientific mechanism.
