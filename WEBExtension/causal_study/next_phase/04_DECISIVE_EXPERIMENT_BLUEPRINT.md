## Hypothesis and causal object

Working hypothesis: a finalizer's selection accuracy depends on the assignment of agent-provided citation addresses even when the candidate answer multiset and source contents are fixed. The directional hypothesis is recorded-binding EM minus exchanged-binding EM>0. It is scientifically possible for the effect to be zero or negative because an original citation can already be irrelevant and a swap can improve alignment.

Primary estimand: mean question-level difference in official answer exact match, `EM(recorded) − EM(exchanged)`, on the prospective gold-free mechanically eligible population, averaged over the prespecified balanced candidate-order and swap schedule. It is the effect of a defined mapping intervention, not an effect of all source grounding, source correctness, live retrieval, internal model reasoning, or learned routing. A positive difference alone cannot establish verified source use.

The randomization/analysis unit is the unique question. Each question is evaluated once under both arms. Candidate permutation and AB/BA execution order are assigned independently by prespecified hashed schedules. One question is not six independent permutations. Actual repeated assignments, if later authorized, must be averaged or modeled within question; they are not part of this minimal pilot.

Exclusions: fewer than two substantive strings; insufficient valid source addresses; no admissible degree-preserving swap; duplicate IDs; changed cache/source checksums; source text mismatch; context/token ceiling breach; or exact prompt-token inequality. Reference and full-source validity need independent blind review before any claim that the swap damages genuine evidence. No currently inspected state is an independently semantic-qualified unit.

A future confirmation uses fresh official-source questions excluded from known prior splits and E6, generates three initial proposals using the frozen original evidence protocol, then applies the same gold-free eligibility screen. ## Treatments and exact controls

ArmR: candidates cite their recorded, sender-visible source sentence addresses.

ArmX: deterministically choose two citation slots from different substantive candidates with different targets and exchange those targets. The complete registry retains the same source IDs, titles, original sentence indices and verbatim text. Candidate answer strings, candidate ordering, citation counts, each source's citation multiplicity, bank ordering, instructions and finalizer settings are identical. No title is relabeled, no fact is transplanted, no source sentence is clipped, and no source is withheld. Reject an exchange that duplicates an already-cited target in a candidate. The graph mutation changes at least two associations, while preserving all candidate outdegrees and source indegrees.

Source selection is the union of valid sentences actually cited in the three initial requests, verified against the original request-local evidence and saved benchmark text. Sorting and swap selection use independent hashes. No annotated supporting fact, answer key, candidate accuracy, confidence or treatment effect selects a source. Every citation resolves in the common bank. Source existence is not entailment. The intervention has no irrelevant-source arm because source text is unchanged; it does not test informative versus irrelevant content.

Opaque source IDs are internal references displayed identically in the bank. Original and exchanged packets must have exactly equal pinned-tokenizer prompt lengths. Source-ID renaming with all relations preserved is a local invariance test, not a paid arm. The only model-visible differences are two candidate `source_ids` entries. Gold labels, arm names, source hashes, old scores and provider keys are excluded by payload allowlists.

The finalizer receives only the question, three frozen answer candidates with unverified source links, and the shared source bank. It chooses one candidate ID. The output cannot introduce a new answer. Candidate IDs are randomized positional labels; no confidence values or peer explanations are invented. All arms use the same frozen Azure `gpt-oss-120b` alias, temperature0, max_tokens2048 and context limit32768 if separately authorized. The lower output cap is a prospective finalizer-only choice, not a measured guarantee against truncation; raw truncations spend quota and stop rather than regenerate.

## Outcomes, statistics and multiplicity

Primary endpoint: question-level official answer EM on the chosen frozen candidate. Primary test: two-sided exact McNemar test on paired EM outcomes, alpha0.05, with a paired question-bootstrap interval for the mean difference. The test targets exchangeability of discordant paired outcomes under the null in the defined population; account for balanced execution order and independence across questions. There is one primary comparison. No p-value or effect is available before paid execution.

Essential secondary diagnostics: paired official F1, selected candidate changes, output validity, baseline exact-proposal coverage (oracle diagnostic ceiling only), substantive-vs-surface-form conflict labels, and blind source/citation support classifications. No new judges or rationales are required. Secondary results are descriptive with intervals; if a later preregistration makes them formal hypotheses, apply Holm correction across that declared family. Pre-treatment correctness/support subgroups may describe heterogeneity but must not be silently promoted to primary or used for outcome-based selection. Never condition on post-treatment answer changes to estimate a population effect.

A selector cannot be correct when every frozen candidate is incorrect. Report the candidate-availability ceiling without using gold to select a practical output. Abstention-aware initial voting is a zero-additional-call contextual baseline, not a substitute for the two finalizer arms or an additional independent observation.

## Power, feasibility and interpretation

The eight-question E6 interaction variance is not the variance of this paired binary selection outcome. Power must vary prospective effect and discordance rates and simulate the exact planned McNemar decision rule. Report small pilot and80%/90% confirmation separately; add screening cost and fresh-pool constraints. The source-capable fresh pool differs from the old complementarity-qualified pool, and neither is a count of eligible conflicting states.

Support for a narrow association effect requires valid unchanged-content manipulation, complete accounting, an adequately powered paired estimate, and replication on fresh states. A claim about **reliable source-grounded selection** additionally needs locked independent judgments that original mappings are better aligned than exchanged ones and a substantive novelty argument against the closest attribution/readout studies. Negative EM difference suggests original links may mislead or swaps may help in this protocol; it does not show evidence is useless. A null with wide intervals is unresolved. No equivalence or noninferiority follows from nonsignificance. Choice changes alone can reflect ordinary decoding variation and do not establish internal reasoning; no claim of deterministic identical generations is made from temperature0.

The result cannot establish natural corruption prevalence, current Web authenticity, a superior learned topology, a general model property, or first-ever provenance sensitivity. The static Wikipedia snapshots and injected target exchange are explicit limits. Given unresolved annotations, close prior overlap and a tiny reused conflict pool, the present decision is **REVISE DESIGN**, not approval to spend.
