# Adversarial review

**Decision: REVISE DESIGN.** Local mechanics are reviewable and tested. A new Web-specific mechanism and a feasible confirmatory population are not established.

Review method: the lead agent applied the three requested reviewer perspectives below after implementation and inspected the saved code, packets, mocks and statistics. Earlier separate agents supplied raw-transition, source and literature analysis. Two of those agents reached a Codex usage limit after saving their work; three independent postimplementation agent reviews were not completed. The following are explicitly lead-agent role-based self-reviews, not three independent replications. Independent code/statistical review remains a condition before a GO decision.

## Reviewer 1: causal validity

**Major objection1: the proposed swap does not necessarily damage evidence.** Original citations have address validity but not adjudicated entailment. An exchange can make a previously irrelevant citation useful. Therefore a recorded-minus-exchanged effect is not automatically the causal benefit of correct grounding. Correction already made: label the estimand citation-association sensitivity throughout; retain a possible negative direction; report original and exchanged support judgments separately. Correction still required for a directional integrity claim: two independent blinded reviewers must classify question/reference validity, semantic conflicts, and support of both mappings, with adjudication before paid outcomes. Keep unresolved cases unresolved and freeze any qualification rule prospectively.

**Major objection2: the comparison cannot establish hypothesisB in full.** Both arms expose every selected source sentence. It does not compare source evidence with answer strings or explanations, and cannot establish that evidence availability improves selection. Correction: state that this is a narrower necessary precursor about relational metadata. A strongB claim is outside its estimand. Do not add multiple helpful-context arms to rescue a weak novelty argument after outcomes arrive.

**Major objection3: fixed text does not fix computation or internal use.** Equal prompt-token counts and the same requested output cap do not force equal hidden reasoning length, effective attention or actual output usage. Temperature0 does not guarantee identical provider generations. Correction already made: preserve only the controlled input and requested compute; meter actual usage and treat choice changes as descriptive. Correction still required before a mechanistic interpretation: establish external behavioral robustness on fresh questions and assess whether ordinary generation variation could explain observed choice changes. No internal-reasoning claim is permitted by the minimum two-arm design.

**Major objection4: selection is narrow and partly post hoc.** The 20 cached states were inspected. At least two normalized substantive strings need not mean two distinct propositions. Selected cited snippets may omit context. Correction already made: the 12-question manifest is exploratory, chosen without gold/treatment results, with all screening decisions saved. Future confirmation must use fresh source questions, the frozen initial protocol and the same gold-free mechanical filter. Correction still required: blind semantic labels and a validated source selection policy. Do not filter on a correct candidate, finalizer success, post-treatment agreement or treatment effect.

**Major objection5: old transition accounting is not mediation.** The 23 preserved-coverage gains,2 newly-covered gains and 1 lost-coverage loss are descriptive. Broadcast can change answer frequencies, contents and selected citations simultaneously. Correction already made: reject a selection-only causal story; include the stronger zero-call readout; distinguish the 20 substantive conflicts from 141 disagreements including abstentions. Do not use post-treatment coverage to claim a causal subgroup effect.

**Resolution:** the current two-target intervention has a clear, narrow input contrast. The parent source-grounding and accessibility mechanisms remain unresolved. No GO recommendation.

## Reviewer 2: WWW scientific significance

**Major objection6: known results cover the broad narrative.** [When Debate Helps](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04686v1) already separates fixed proposal supply and verification-aware readout. RAG, asymmetry and provenance studies in report 03 are direct neighbors. The refreshed [GAVEL paper](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1789/) further crowds evidence-binding debate. Correction already made:13 primary papers compared; generic utilization, asymmetry and first-provenance claims rejected. Correction still required: articulate an externally meaningful failure that the narrower edge reassignment identifies beyond those papers. A different dataset, degree preservation or an extra implementation detail alone is insufficient.

**Major objection7: no natural failure rate is measured.** Injecting citation target swaps shows a constructed perturbation's effect, not that provenance corruption naturally explains past broadcast gains or harms. Static Wikipedia passages do not test publisher trust, live source freshness, Web search changes or adversarial prevalence. Correction: restrict the target to Web-derived, multi-source synthesis in the static protocol. Before claiming practical significance, independently measure a relevant natural source-association failure in a separate blinded audit. That measurement is currently absent.

**Major objection8: the strongest finding may be an evaluation/readout artifact.** Excluding explicit abstentions raises partitioned exact matches from 10 to 62 without inference, versus 34 after broadcast. Spending to recover a known available candidate may mainly compensate for the original voting rule. Correction already made: make this baseline central, retain its contradictory evidence and exploratory status, and do not claim communication is necessary to use available answers. The 20-case conflict contrast is too imprecise to rescue a general benefit. Prospective baseline validation can be part of the same future study without extra model calls.

**Resolution:** an evidence-led audit is defensible now; a competitive WWW mechanism contribution is conditional. If the only distinction remains a controlled metadata swap already subsumed by close attribution work, stop that novelty narrative rather than expanding inference.

## Reviewer 3: reproducibility and statistical rigor

**Major objection9: the cached sample is not a powered confirmation.** The 12-question pilot reuses exposed questions and cannot estimate a small population effect precisely. The eight-question E6 F1 interaction is a different outcome. Correction already made: paired binary power scenarios, exact McNemar decision rule, question-level clustering, no E6-variance extrapolation, and no mock accuracy claim. One primary test is planned; secondary metrics stay descriptive. Prespecify a minimum important effect before funding, rather than choosing it to fit the available pool.

**Major objection10: screening cost can make the study infeasible.** At20/300 yield, finding825 eligible questions needs about 12,375 screened source questions and 38,775 completions under the point assumptions. Only 6,809 source-capable fresh questions are currently available, before semantic qualification. Correction already made: count three proposal generations per screened question, including failures; separate those from two selector calls per accepted case; refine the coarse power grid. Correction still required: a credible fresh-yield estimate and an absolute screening ceiling. Wilson uncertainty covers binomial sampling, not distribution shift or adjudication losses. A finite-pool yield is not guaranteed.

**Major objection11: execution provenance and failure handling can bias results.** An execution-mode string alone is not authorization. Incomplete pairs can depend on arm; treating them as wrong or silently dropping them changes the estimand. Correction already made: unconditional paid block, source/configuration hashes, old-ledger checks, separate mock ledger, durable caches, raw artifacts, strict parsing, missing-cell rejection, and all-packet audit. Correction still required before live use: independently review a separately authorized live entry point, authenticate output provenance and lock its failure/missingness analysis. Current checkpoint hashes protect against accidental corruption, not malicious replacement.

**Major objection12: model and cost replication remain incomplete.** The deployment is an alias; a future provider revision can change behavior. New selector latency/output length and deployment-specific price are unknown. The mocked output cap differs from old E6 and may truncate. Correction: record returned model, request settings, provider IDs, timing and token usage; retain the 2,048-token cap as a prospective choice and stop on truncation. Treat E6 output/runtime as reference scenarios and money as unknown. Future live pacing must differ from the zero-delay mock configuration. The current pilot construction caps must be reviewed before any larger study.

**Resolution:**171 tests plus 12 subtests and 24 mock requests establish local mechanical validity. They do not establish provider behavior, independent semantic labels, external reproducibility or novelty. The lack of three independent postimplementation reviews is itself an unresolved replication limitation.
