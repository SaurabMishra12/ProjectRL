## A. Strongest observed finding

The most consequential observation is a weak original aggregation baseline. On the same300 saved partitioned questions, a fixed gold-free abstention-aware vote over unchanged initial proposals returns **62 exact matches**, versus **10** for the original vote and **34** after broadcast. It excludes normalized `noanswer` and empty explicit provider refusals, then uses the original majority and node-order tie rule; all-abstaining cases return `noanswer`. It uses no gold labels, confidence, new sources or inference.

Relative to the original vote, that readout gains 52 questions and loses 0: EM+17.333 points, paired question-bootstrap95% CI[13.000,21.667]; F1+23.823 points, CI[19.202,28.344]. Broadcast relative to the stronger readout gains 5 and loses 33: EM−9.333 points, CI[−13.333,−5.333]; F1−13.890 points, CI[−17.903,−9.890]. This is an exploratory post hoc comparison on existing selected questions, not a preregistered population result or proof that communication always harms.

The original10→34 improvement is fully accounted for:25 gains and 1 loss;23 gains retain correct-proposal coverage,2 gain correct coverage, and the loss loses coverage. Coverage67→66 hides5 gained and 6 lost question memberships. Broadcast final answers were already present initially on 293/300 questions, including32/34 correct finals. Revision can change proposal multiplicity, correct or destroy answers, and transfer citations. These traces do not isolate evidence-backed selection or a fixed-proposal causal effect.

## B. Strongest unproven hypothesis

The narrow candidate is **source-association sensitivity during selection**: assigning a source passage to a different candidate can change answer selection even when candidate answers and all available source text are held fixed. This matters for synthesis systems that forward answer claims with pointers to independently retrieved sources. A recipient may rely on an incorrect pointer despite having the underlying text available.

It remains a hypothesis. No new selector outcomes exist. Original citations are not independently verified as supporting, so exchanging them need not reduce support. A positive recorded-minus-exchanged effect would establish a behavioral effect of that mapping intervention, not faithful internal source use. A negative effect could reflect misleading original links or improved links after exchange. A wide null is unresolved. A sufficiently precise near-zero effect would constrain the mapping effect in this protocol, while leaving the value of source content unresolved.

HypothesisA is consistent with descriptive proposal accounting but too broad and redundant as novelty. GenericB is plausible but not isolated by old traces; this design tests only its narrower relational precursor. HypothesisC remains unresolved because annotated support placement is not semantic necessity. Among the old 300 source unions,224 lack at least one annotated support; their absence does not prove no alternative answer path. No current E6 case is independently necessity-qualified. See [report 02](/home/saurab/Documents/projectRLMAIN/WEBExtension/causal_study/next_phase/02_SOURCE_AND_NECESSITY_AUDIT.md).

## C. What is genuinely novel?

**No genuinely novel mechanism is established by this phase.** The strongest result is a useful research audit, not a new general theory or method. [When Debate Helps](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04686v1) directly precedes the proposal-supply/readout account. [Attribution Bias](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.1087/) already studies fixed documents with changed metadata. Citation faithfulness and laundering studies, together with [GAVEL](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1789/), further narrow any provenance claim. The 13-paper comparison, source versions, inspected sections and bounded search limits are in [report 03](/home/saurab/Documents/projectRLMAIN/WEBExtension/causal_study/next_phase/03_NOVELTY_GAP_AND_RELATED_WORK.md).

Exchanging two existing candidate–source edges while preserving all text, candidate multiplicities and graph degrees is an operational distinction. It is not by itself a substantive scientific advance. A credible contribution would require a meaningful failure of evidence association that those neighbors do not already establish, independent semantic characterization and prospective evidence. If that distinction cannot be articulated, stop the provenance-novelty narrative; do not spend to rename a known effect.

## D. Exactly one next primary design

Conditional recommendation: **paired recorded-versus-exchanged citation-target selection on frozen proposals and a common unchanged source bank**. The fresh confirmatory version uses unexposed source questions, three frozen initial proposals per screened question and the prespecified gold-free eligibility rule. Every accepted question receives one finalizer call per arm. Exchange exactly two citation targets across different substantive candidates, preserving per-candidate citation counts, per-source multiplicity, answer strings, source contents/order, candidate order, instructions and exact prompt-token count.

Primary endpoint is paired official answer EM, tested once with two-sided exact McNemar at 0.05 and reported with question-level paired intervals. The finalizer chooses only C0/C1/C2. Essential secondary diagnostics are F1, output validity, choice changes, the frozen candidate oracle ceiling and blind semantic source/conflict labels. The abstention-aware readout remains a zero-call comparator. No paid judge, invented explanation, new rationale stage or automatic E6 rerun is included. See the full estimand, exclusions, failure policy and interpretation in [report 04](/home/saurab/Documents/projectRLMAIN/WEBExtension/causal_study/next_phase/04_DECISIVE_EXPERIMENT_BLUEPRINT.md).

The cached12-question version is a mechanism-validation pilot of this same design, not a second confirmatory experiment. It reuses exposed states and cannot establish population effects or novelty. The four-arm helpful-context design was rejected; necessity-qualified accessibility was deferred. Both are documented alternatives, not additional experiments recommended for immediate funding.

## G. Recommended WWW2027 story

1. **Working title:** *Auditing Answer Aggregation and Source Links in Multi-Agent Web QA*.
2. **Research problem:** Multi-agent Web-derived QA combines answers, abstentions and citations from agents with different local evidence. A higher collective score can result from changes in voting, candidate availability, answer revision or source association. Without strong readout baselines and independently assessed source support, these explanations are easily conflated. The research problem is to determine which of these observable mechanisms explains behavior and whether an evidence-association intervention changes decisions after candidate and source contents are fixed.
3. **Main hypothesis:** Some selection failures arise from candidate–source association rather than missing source contents; a rigorously qualified mapping contrast could isolate that behavioral dependence. This is unproven and its distinctness remains conditional.
4. **Existing evidence:** paired historical transitions, the 62-versus 34 zero-call baseline result, sparse substantive conflicts, source/provenance limitations, and the imprecise eight-question E6 interaction. Each has its own exploratory scope; none is new confirmatory grounding evidence.
5. **Missing causal evidence:** an independently qualified, fresh, adequately powered intervention with authenticated provider artifacts and a distinct scientific target beyond the closest literature. The current mock study supplies no empirical answer.
6. **Expected contribution if confirmed:** a bounded behavioral account of how evidence associations affect fixed-proposal selection, plus a reproducible protocol that separates aggregation, source access, source support and assigned provenance. Whether that merits WWW depends on substantive novelty and practical relevance, not merely a significant p-value.
7. **Alternative interpretation if not confirmed:** the finalizer may recover support directly from the unchanged bank, ignore pointers, or be insensitive within the tested population. A precise null would delimit this mechanism. Meanwhile the present audit may chiefly reveal abstention-sensitive voting and unsuitable necessity assumptions, a useful negative/replication outcome rather than a novel communication result.
8. **Explicit limitations:** exposed cached states; selected benchmark questions; missing independent semantic labels; possible reference defects and alternative evidence; lexical rather than semantic conflict screening; artificial target exchanges; unverified original citations; sparse eligibility; unmeasured new decoding behavior; model alias drift; fixed Wikipedia snapshots; one primary model/protocol; incomplete independent review; and close prior art. No abstract asserting an unobserved positive result is warranted.
