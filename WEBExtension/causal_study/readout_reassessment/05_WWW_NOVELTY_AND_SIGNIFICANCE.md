# WWW novelty and significance

**Decision: no novel WWW mechanism is established. A findings/evaluation pivot is more defensible than the current SparseComm superiority narrative.** This is a bounded primary-source review checked 16 October 2026, not an exhaustive novelty certificate. Prior reported experiments were inspected, not reproduced. Original full-text methods were used where available; proceedings and author records establish identities and venues. OpenReview's Diagnostic Study page presented a browser challenge, so its official PMLR record and author-hosted paper were used.

## Closest required papers

**When Debate Helps — Zhao et al.** First preprint 3 October 2026, arXiv 2610.04686v1; no conference venue verified. Sections 2–3 and 5 distinguish proposal supply, recoverable headroom and verification-aware readout, with controlled fixed-proposal evidence probes. Generic discovery that a correct minority can be available while the final vote is wrong is therefore redundant. Our explicit abstention deletion and crossed E5 readouts are a particular evaluation intervention, not a new proposal-utilization theory. [Original paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04686v1).

**Debate or Vote — Choi, Zhu and Sharon Li.** NeurIPS 2025; inspected arXiv 2508.17536v2. Sections 3–5 compare voting and interaction and test preservation/update rules. These are direct precedents for separating ensemble benefits from revision and for tracking correct answers lost during debate. The paper's martingale/model assumptions are not a universal impossibility result for evidence-informed communication. It does not directly establish this study's abstention-by-readout interaction. [Original paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.17536v2); [official proceedings](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/934252acd87f254d5d4672fbde283bd2-Abstract-Conference.html).

**Beyond Majority Voting — Ai, Pan, Simchi-Levi, Tambe and Xu.** ICML 2026, PMLR 306:1311–1340; inspected arXiv 2510.01499v2. Sections 2–5 develop aggregation using accuracy and higher-order prediction information under stated assumptions. Thus aggregation quality and agent dependence are established research objects. Our gold-free deletion rule does not estimate those reliabilities and does not improve that theory. The conditional-independence extension still has structured assumptions; it is not a claim about arbitrary correlated agents. [Official record](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/ai26d.html); [original full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.01499v2).

**Free-MAD — Cui, Fu, Zhang, Wang and Zuo.** First preprint 14 September 2025; FindingsACL 2026, pp 31977–31997. The inspected original §3 separates debate from decision; §4.3 and Algorithm 1 score trajectories, including answer adoption/abandonment. Algorithm 1 removes `None` keys and randomly resolves tied maximal scores. Therefore claiming that prior work ignores aggregation, missing responses or tie behavior would be incorrect. Its trajectory mechanism and anti-conformity prompts differ from applying three simple readouts to identical saved final states; neither difference alone establishes novelty. [Official record](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1600/); [inspected original v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.11035v1). The comparison uses that identified version, not an unverified claim that every final-version detail is identical.

**A Diagnostic Study of Multi-Agent LLMs for Real-World Debates — Pitre, Srivastava, Zhang, Wang, Ramakrishnan and Wang.** ICML 2026, PMLR 306:99003–99017. Sections 3–4 distinguish deliberative-process diagnostics from correctness, with targeted pathological behaviors and objective/subjective settings. This directly precedes a generic claim that agreement is not reasoning quality. Its primary construct is deliberation quality, not a controlled abstention/readout contrast on Web-derived QA. [Official record](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/pitre26a.html); [inspected author full text](https://people.cs.vt.edu/naren/papers/Diagnostic_MultiAgentDebate.pdf).

## Abstention, evidence sufficiency and RAG evaluation

**Snorkel — Ratner et al.** Original preprint 2017; expanded VLDB Journal article online 2019, issue 2020. The inspected labeling-function formulation explicitly separates labels and abstentions and models source accuracy/correlation. Treating abstention as absence of a class vote is longstanding ensemble practice. It is a conceptual antecedent, not evidence that every QA plurality comparison has already been audited. [Primary author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7075849/).

**Sufficient Context — Joren et al.** ICLR 2025; first preprint 9 November 2024, inspected 2411.06037v3 dated 23 April 2025. Sections 3–5 distinguish context sufficiency from model success and study selective generation, including HotpotQA. This directly precedes the claim that retrieved relevance or annotated support is not sufficient to identify useful answer information. Its autorater is a model-based sufficiency judgment; neither its labels nor our annotation-overlap proxy should be treated as unquestionable semantic truth. [Author/institution venue record](https://research.google/pubs/sufficient-context-a-new-lens-on-retrieval-augmented-generation-systems-2/); [original full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.06037v3).

**UAEval 4RAG — Peng, Choubey, Xiong and Wu.** ACL 2025, pp 8452–8472; first preprint 16 December 2024. Its knowledge-base-specific unanswerability taxonomy and rejection/acceptance evaluation are direct precedents for measuring refusals alongside answer accuracy. This weakens a generic evaluation-novelty claim based on adding an abstention column. It does not establish our particular communication-by-readout contrast. [Official record](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.415/); [original full text inspected](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.12300v2).

**GRAB-RAG — Yohanes Andre Setiawan.** Preprint 23August2026, inspected 2608.22228v1; no peer-reviewed venue verified. Section 3 compares supportive, degraded, missing and misleading contexts, with matched question identities, five passage slots and multiple abstention policies on NQ/HotpotQA. Its constructions use annotated passages/answer substitutions, so they are not a gold-free deployment policy. This is close prior evidence that evidence quality and abstention interact; our observed annotation-overlap strata do not advance it causally. [Original paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.22228v1).

**MM-AQA — Madhusudhan, Yadav and Lacoste.** Preprint 16 April 2026,2604.14799v1; no venue independently verified. It studies evidence insufficiency and abstention in multimodal models and multi-agent architectures, including strict-majority self-consistency abstention. This is a direct counterexample to claiming that multi-agent abstention trade-offs are new. The modalities and evaluation targets differ from our answer-only Web QA reaggregation. [Original paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.14799v1).

The proposed source-association narrative also faces the attribution/provenance precedents already inspected in the preserved [next-phase literature review](/home/saurab/Documents/projectRLMAIN/WEBExtension/causal_study/next_phase/03_NOVELTY_GAP_AND_RELATED_WORK.md), notably fixed-content authorship interventions, citation faithfulness/laundering and evidence-binding debate. No new novelty claim is inferred by failing to find a paper with exactly our title or graph mutation.

## What the present study adds, and what it does not

The concrete observation is that **the estimated communication gain depends sharply on a task-compatible readout in this saved system**. Partitioned Broadcast−NoComm changes from+8 to+1 EM points; the lexical tie sensitivity gives 0. The original readout lets abstentions override a substantive candidate, and changing it helps NoComm more than Broadcast. This is a clean offline intervention on the deterministic readout of fixed responses. It is not a randomized intervention on evidence scarcity, not proof of internal source use, and not evidence that communication generally harms.

There is a simple structural reason to expect improvement in this particular answer-only metric: if all abstention outputs score 0, deleting abstentions from the specified plurality preserves every original substantive winner and can only replace an originally wrong abstention. A large readout improvement is therefore not, by itself, a surprising general mechanism. What could be scientifically valuable is a reproducible demonstration that published system rankings or quality–cost conclusions are unstable under compatible readouts across independently implemented Web QA systems, including genuinely unanswerable requests and semantic source labels.

The specific joint pattern of evidence scarcity, abstention multiplicity and apparent communication benefit is not causally established here, and the reviewed papers do not settle every version of that joint question. Their substantial overlap means we cannot elevate this implementation-dependent counterexample into a novel WWW claim. A protocol, a robust cross-system empirical regularity, or a precisely demonstrated external failure could be a contribution; a repaired benchmark baseline alone is presently an evaluation flaw/audit finding.

## Narrative comparison

**A — Aggregation-sensitive communication gains.** Directly observed through all 20 arms and three matched readouts. Not established across datasets, backbones, independently implemented systems or genuinely unanswerable tasks. Closest neighbors are the five required debate/readout papers and weak-supervision aggregation. It matters when Web QA products compare communicating agents against an incompatible abstention baseline. Minimum next evidence is independent saved-trace replication and an externally reviewed readout/coverage protocol, costing 0 API calls where compatible traces exist. A later fresh paired initial/private/Broadcast replication costs 9N calls per backbone, with no conflict-screening filter. The 6,809-question source-capable pool is adequate for ordinary broad-cohort samples, not a power guarantee. Contribution potential: plausible as findings/evaluation work, but uncertain and currently insufficient.

**B — Scarcity changes aggregation behavior.** Annotated-support availability and abstention patterns covary; shared and partitioned local contexts also differ. Semantic insufficiency, causal necessity and a scarcity-mediated communication effect are unproven. Closest neighbors are Sufficient Context, UAEval 4RAG, GRAB-RAG and MM-AQA. The practical issue is distinguishing retrieval failure, justified abstention and aggregation failure in Web synthesis. Minimum next work is blinded semantic source annotation plus a feasible independently specified intervention;0 API calls now. No paid scarcity experiment is recommended until its estimand and qualified cohort exist. The 6,809 count does not establish such eligibility. Contribution potential: unresolved; stronger construct validation is required before treating it as more than known sufficiency/abstention behavior.

**C — Evidence–proposal association.** No new causal outcome has been observed. The cached 20 conflicts and 24-call pilot are selected, unqualified and close to attribution literature. A small pilot could measure this protocol's pointer sensitivity but would not resolve novelty, semantic integrity or the 825-question feasibility problem. Recommendation: abandon the currently prepared study as the next primary WWW experiment, preserving all artifacts. Calls now:0; no fresh qualified population established. Contribution potential for this current proposal: low, with uncertainty about other independently motivated provenance questions.

Recommended direction: **REVISE/pivot toward an externally validated findings/evaluation study. Stop presenting the present results as an established sparse-communication mechanism.** Do not fund a larger sample merely to sharpen an already ambiguous scientific claim.
