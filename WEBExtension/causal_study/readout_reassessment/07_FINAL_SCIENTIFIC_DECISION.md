# Final scientific decision

**REVISE — pivot toward an independently validated findings/evaluation study.** The present evidence does not justify an established sparse-communication mechanism or a paid source-association pilot. Stop that primary mechanism narrative; preserve the data and the genuine descriptive quality–cost trade-offs. New Azure completions, paid judges and training runs in this phase: **0**.

## Question 1: does Broadcast still outperform NoComm under the same abstention-aware rule?

**Its partitioned point estimate is higher, but a reliable general advantage is not established.** Both conditions use the same three-response readout. Broadcast returns 65/300 exact matches and NoComm 62/300: **+1.000 EM point**,8 paired wins,5 losses,287 ties, exact two-sided McNemar p=0.5811, paired 95% CI[−1.333,+3.333]. F1 is 30.113 versus 28.882: **+1.231 points**, CI[−0.802,+3.389]. Under the additional abstention-aware lexical tie rule, both have 64/300 exact matches; F1 differs by+0.232 points, CI[−1.711,+2.195]. Shared-evidence abstention-aware EM is 73/300 for both.

Therefore the earlier comparison of 62 correct NoComm answers with 34 correct Broadcast answers **cannot establish that Broadcast harms accuracy** when the readouts differ. These reports replace that interpretation with the matched comparison while leaving every historical artifact untouched. Nonsignificance also does not establish equivalence or absence of a useful population effect.

## Question 2: does learned SparseComm retain a meaningful quality–cost advantage?

**Some frozen policies retain favorable descriptive trade-offs; a general meaningful advantage is unproven.** In partitioned evidence, learned seeds 7/19/31 yield 65/65/63 exact matches with the common abstention-aware rule, versus 62 NoComm and 65 Broadcast. Seed 19 references 1,200 historical successful request keys versus 1,800 for Broadcast: one-third fewer, the same exact-match count, and F1−0.166 points with paired CI[−1.597,+1.229]. Equal point estimates and an interval spanning zero are not a noninferiority/equivalence demonstration. Seed 7 saves only 7/1,800 requests and reproduces Broadcast's scored outcomes; seed 31 is cheaper but has 2 fewer correct answers.

In shared evidence, seeds 7/19/31 give 78/77/72 correct versus 73 NoComm,75 Static and 73 Broadcast. Their referenced successful request counts are 1,382/1,239/959 versus 1,800 for Broadcast. Those trade-offs are real observations, but the quality estimates are small and seed-dependent, not independent replications or a per-question ensemble. Selecting seed 19 or any other policy after looking at this test set would introduce selection bias.

MatchedRandom shares edge count and quota with each learned policy but often differs in recipient-update calls. Its comparison is a system-policy trade-off, not an isolated topology effect under equal inference resources. Actual per-arm request references and token lower bounds are reported, and training/selection costs are not hidden as test-serving costs. A new universal learned-routing advantage cannot be inferred from these 20 arms.

## Question 3: were previous gains substantially driven by weak readout?

**Yes, in the observed partitioned comparison.** With the original readout, Broadcast−NoComm is+8.000 EM points (34−10 correct). With the same abstention-aware readout on both it is+1.000 (65−62). The readout-by-communication contrast is **−7.000 points**, paired 95% CI[−10.333,−4.000]. The observed point gain attenuates **87.5%**. F1 attenuates from+9.932 to+1.231 points, a difference of−8.701, CI[−12.295,−5.341]. These are exploratory arithmetic contrasts, not the percentage of a causally isolated mediator.

Repairing abstention handling adds 52 correct NoComm endpoints and 31 Broadcast endpoints on fixed proposals. The differential 21 questions accounts for the 24→3 net communication-gain change. The rule excludes only explicit abstentions and retains the original substantive winner/tie policy. Every defined abstention receives zero answer EM against all 300 selected reference labels. This structural compatibility issue does not prove that excluding abstentions is safe on genuinely unanswerable Web requests.

Static and learned-policy effects are fully reassessed in report 03. For example, partitioned Static's point effect changes from+1.000 to−0.667EM points, while learned seeds 7/19/31 change from+8.000/+3.333/+2.333 to+1.000/+1.000/+0.333. These are changes in exploratory point estimates, not statistically established sign reversals.

## Question 4: novel finding or evaluation flaw?

**Currently an implementation-dependent evaluation/readout flaw plus a useful empirical audit.** Prior work already separates debate and readout, improves aggregation, distinguishes abstention from labels and studies RAG sufficiency. The present analysis does not establish a new general causal mechanism. Its independent implementation reproduces every original E5 score and applies all three readouts to all 20 arms on the same 300 questions, making the counterexample reproducible and reviewable. That is worthwhile evidence, but not sufficient novelty for WWW.

The strongest possible extension is a reproducible, externally validated protocol showing when Web QA rankings and quality–cost conclusions depend on incompatible abstention/readout semantics. It requires independent implementations/datasets/backbones and explicit answerability, source-support and physical-cost evaluation. No acceptance probability or guaranteed conference contribution is claimed. See the primary-literature comparison in report 05.

## Question 5: smallest genuinely necessary additional experiment?

**First, zero new inference:** independent compatible saved-trace replication, blinded semantic source/reference review, and external review of the evaluation contract. IncreasingN in this same implementation would not by itself establish generality or novelty.

If that work supports a distinct findings claim, one conditional fresh confirmation uses 3 initial+3 private+3 Broadcast calls per question, then computes every locked readout offline: **9N completions per backbone**. Its primary readout interaction compares Broadcast with the call-count-matched private revision control; initial NoComm remains available for free. There is no conflict/correctness-based selection. The independently verified 6,809-question broad source-capable pool avoids the 20/300 conflict-screening bottleneck. A powered sample and actual tariff remain unresolved; no fixed full-scale budget is justified. The 20-question/180-call example is an operational scale only, not a WWW confirmation or authorization.

**Do not run the prepared 24-call source-association pilot.** Abandon it as the next primary WWW experiment because semantic integrity, natural prevalence, prior distinctness and larger-study feasibility are unresolved. Preserve all of its artifacts.

## Question 6: continue, pivot or stop?

**Pivot, with REVISE status.** Continue the evidence-led aggregation/evaluation investigation only if independent zero-call replication supports a meaningful external failure. Stop promoting the current data as a demonstrated novel sparse-communication or source-association mechanism. If independent traces do not show a transferable issue or the closest literature subsumes the evaluation contribution, stop pursuing this mechanism for WWW instead of purchasing a larger positive-looking result.

Working title: *When Readout Choices Change Communication Gains in Web QA*. A defensible current claim is: “In one saved multi-agent Web-derived QA evaluation, identical abstention-aware reaggregation reduces the partitioned Broadcast−NoComm EM gain from 8 to 1 point; some learned policies retain descriptive quality–cost trade-offs, but their general advantage is unestablished.” This is not an abstract asserting an unobserved new mechanism.

These are nonadditive cross-arm references, not new calls or exclusive per-arm spend. Every historical artifact and checkpoint in the preservation baseline remains protected.

All statistical results are post hoc. The question is the resampling unit; seeds, arms and readouts are not additional independent samples. Main intervals use 10,000 paired question bootstraps; smaller exhaustive diagnostic strata use 2,000. Exact paired EM tests and declared-family Holm values are retained, but p-values do not validate selecting this readout after inspection. Semantic source sufficiency/entailment remains unresolved; annotated-support absence is only a proxy. One backbone, one selected question set, existing decoding realizations, static Wikipedia evidence, policy selection under the old reward and incomplete usage bound generalization.
