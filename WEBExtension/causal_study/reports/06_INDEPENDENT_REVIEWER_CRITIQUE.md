# Independent reviewer challenge

Review date: 15 October 2026. This review challenges identification and interpretation, rather than trying to make the paper appear favorable. The initial review preceded paid inference. The final addendum below was checked against the paid status, usage, statistical outputs, mechanism-event register, and the relevant raw question traces. Mock answers are never scientific observations.

## Scope and evidence

The reviewed protocol is E6 with eight unique questions, two placements, three frozen initial responses per placement, and a matched private-revision versus fixed broadcast-revision comparison. It requires 18 successful completions per question, 144 in total. E7 paid inference is deferred. Broadcast carries initial peer answer proposals and valid cited source sentences on six directed edges. It does not guarantee delivery of every missing supporting fact.

The primary estimand is the question mean of `(distributed_comm − distributed_nocomm) − (colocated_comm − colocated_nocomm)`, for this particular proposal-and-cited-evidence broadcast protocol. `design.py`, `provider.py`, `run.py`, and `statistics.py` were read directly. The graph's 2026-10-13T17:28:45Z generation does not track these new files; its empty results were not treated as evidence of absence. The reviewer also inspected `offline/e8_report.md` and the targeted primary references in `literature_notes.md`.

## Objections and smallest corrections

### 1. Extra revision computation in the original 12-call design

**Severity: critical. Evidence:** initial NoComm versus updated Comm changes both peer access and the number of model updates. **Current resolution:** corrected before outcomes: both revision arms receive three updates from the same placement-specific frozen initial state, with identical model settings, instructions, and majority aggregation. The revised budget is eight questions × 18 calls. **Smallest correction:** retain this matched private revision baseline; report initial majority only as a secondary zero-extra-call anchor. **Additional API need:** the corrected pilot uses 144 rather than the original suggested 120; no further calls are needed to repair this design once executed.

### 2. The intervention does not isolate genuinely novel source information

**Severity: major for a mechanism claim. Evidence:** `design.revision_messages` supplies peer answers together with cited evidence; no citation implies an answer-only peer message. Answer copying, source exposure, longer context, and social agreement can all affect revision. **Current resolution:** the estimand explicitly concerns the bundled fixed broadcast protocol. Missing-support delivery and answer transitions are observed diagnostics, not proof of internal evidence use. **Smallest correction:** narrow the E6 claim; retain E7 as a separate frozen-receiver source-passage intervention. **Additional API need:** none for honest E6 reporting; a future E7 test would need separately approved paired receiver updates after semantic control validation.

### 3. Controlled gold-support placement is an artificial, selected population

**Severity: major for natural-retrieval claims. Evidence:** eligibility requires both annotated supporting articles among the supplied candidates, and gold annotations construct the six-document pool. Existing natural candidate sets frequently omit an annotated article. **Current resolution:** model payloads use source text without construction labels; the protocol calls the task controlled evidence availability. **Smallest correction:** restrict conclusions to eligible controlled pools; distinguish missing candidates from ranking failure and acknowledge alternative evidence. **Additional API need:** none to limit the claim; realistic retrieval transport requires a separately justified study rather than treating E6 as one.

### 4. Eight questions cannot settle a mean interaction or its variance

**Severity: major. Evidence:** eight question clusters and a bounded, discrete F1 endpoint permit unstable estimates and bootstrap degeneracy. **Current resolution:** `statistics.analyze_e6` labels output exploratory and computes each contrast before resampling whole questions. `power_sensitivity` stresses pilot SD using a 0.25-unit floor and SD multipliers 1, 1.5, and 2; assumed effects are 2, 5, and 10 F1 points rather than the observed pilot mean. **Smallest correction:** use the pilot for feasibility and a prespecified sensitivity analysis; obtain approval before a fresh confirmatory sample. **Additional API need:** no extra pilot calls; the sensitivity grid reports 18 × N completions for fresh confirmatory questions.

### 5. The sign-flip p-value is conditional on an inferential assumption

**Severity: moderate. Evidence:** all four cells are evaluated for each question; communication labels are not physically randomized to independent units. A zero average effect alone does not make signs exchangeable. **Current resolution:** the test explicitly requires independent, symmetric zero-centered question contrasts under the null. It enumerates all signs for at most 20 nonzero contrasts, with two-sided absolute-sum statistics. Larger tests use a plus-one corrected Monte Carlo p-value. **Smallest correction:** state the assumption beside the result and avoid calling this an assumption-free randomization test. **Additional API need:** none.

### 6. Power estimates are approximate and distribution dependent

**Severity: moderate. Evidence:** a pilot cannot identify the population tail behavior. The implemented power simulation uses symmetrized empirical residuals with SD scaling and a conditional-normal approximation to the sign-flip test. Its Monte Carlo interval quantifies simulation error, not pilot-variance uncertainty. Some stress scenarios may exceed the natural interaction support [−2,2], which is reported explicitly. **Current resolution:** outputs are planning sensitivities, not promises of 80% or 90% power. **Smallest correction:** inspect feasibility and validate the chosen sample size with the exact planned test and a plausible bounded data-generating model before confirmatory approval; do not use infeasible scaled scenarios as literal population forecasts. **Additional API need:** none for that computational validation.

### 7. Seeds, placements, and overlapping E1–E5 artifacts are correlated

**Severity: critical if pooled as independent data. Evidence:** E8 summarizes many condition records on the same 300 identities; E2 overlaps E1, and E3-v2 followed inspection of those identities. **Current resolution:** new analysis rejects duplicate question IDs. Separate E8 condition summaries retain 300 as the question denominator, and an optimizer-seed mean remains a summary of policies. **Smallest correction:** collapse repeated assignments within question by a prespecified rule or retain them in a clustered model; never multiply the sample size by agents, arms, rounds, or seeds. **Additional API need:** none.

### 8. Aggregation can dominate final performance

**Severity: major for mechanism claims. Evidence:** offline E5 partitioned NoComm has 67/300 questions with an initially exact candidate but only 10 majority-exact answers; broadcast has 66 revised-any-exact questions and 34 majority-exact answers. Correct available candidates rejected by majority fall from 57 to 32. These observations suggest selection or propagation of existing correct proposals without increased proposal coverage; they do not identify which caused the gains. **Current resolution:** E6 fixes the aggregator and records candidate-level exact match, F1, revision losses, recoveries, and majority rejection. E8 calls oracle scores diagnostic upper bounds. **Smallest correction:** retain the decomposition and do not claim that an oracle selects deployable answers or that a changed majority proves source utilization. **Additional API need:** none for descriptive audit; direct mediation attribution would require a new controlled intervention.

### 9. Equal document counts do not equal equal reading or communication costs

**Severity: moderate. Evidence:** two articles per node can differ greatly in tokens; citing a different number of sentences changes the broadcast payload. **Current resolution:** exact-token evidence and prompt lengths, actual provider input/output usage, directed deliveries, and recipient updates are retained. Equal revision counts control one resource dimension, not all tokens or latency. **Smallest correction:** report realized differences and call the effect a fixed-protocol access effect; avoid attribution to routing efficiency or equal physical compute. **Additional API need:** none.

### 10. Role, ownership, document order, and tie-breaking can matter

**Severity: moderate. Evidence:** specialists have distinct role prompts and three-way majority ties select by fixed node order. Eight questions cannot cover all assignment permutations. **Current resolution:** assignments counterbalance owners and roles, document order varies deterministically, roles are held fixed across each question's paired placements, and tie-breaking is identical in all arms. **Smallest correction:** report the actual balance and ties; limit conclusions to the frozen assignment schedule. Assignment robustness is a future sensitivity rather than additional independent question evidence. **Additional API need:** none for these diagnostics; fresh assignment replicates cost 18 per question and remain clustered.

### 11. Held-out identities do not prove semantic or model-training independence

**Severity: major for confirmation. Evidence:** existing artifacts have been extensively inspected; exact ID/question blocking does not exclude paraphrases, related articles, or pretrained exposure. The deployment exposes an alias rather than an immutable provider snapshot. **Current resolution:** the new sample blocks known exposed IDs and normalized questions and records document-title overlap. The protocol does not claim document disjointness or an unexposed backbone. **Smallest correction:** archive the exact exclusion inventory and returned model metadata; use fresh prespecified holdouts for confirmation and acknowledge unmeasured pretraining contamination. **Additional API need:** none for provenance; any second model or new benchmark requires later authorization.

### 12. Novelty is not established by the broad central hypothesis

**Severity: major. Evidence:** [Shen et al., EMNLP 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.623/) already study causal information/error propagation. [Li et al., July 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01661) explicitly design private/shared evidence for deliberation. [Zhao et al., October 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04686) distinguish correct-proposal supply from verification-aware readout and use fixed-proposal evidence probes. **Current resolution:** the bounded literature check is in `literature_notes.md`; E8 is an audit, not an asserted novel decomposition. **Smallest correction:** compare the exact intervention and assumptions; a possible contribution is the crossed same-union placement study with matched revision and transparent failure accounting, subject to adequate evidence. **Additional API need:** none for novelty assessment. Neither a favorable pilot nor added experiments by themselves predict WWW acceptance.

### 13. Budget and ambiguous-request recovery can invalidate safe execution

**Severity: critical. Evidence:** retries and process restarts can exceed a nominal question budget or regenerate ambiguous requests. **Current resolution:** `provider.BudgetBackend` uses a durable ledger, serial locking, hard 150-completion and 180-attempt gates, no hidden SDK retry layer, 429-only retries, checksummed request/response state, and a global halt on uncertain execution. `run.pilot` uses one fixed paid directory. Mock/provider tests exercise limit and resume behavior. **Smallest correction:** retain one ledger and stop upon uncertainty; reconcile provider-side execution rather than moving to another budget namespace. **Additional API need:** none for safeguards; recovery never authorizes extra requests.

### 14. Frozen artifacts must be verified, not merely named frozen

**Severity: moderate, resolved before inference. Evidence:** the pre-inference review initially found that resume trusted a stored manifest hash without recomputing it or calling assignment validation. **Current resolution:** `run.verify_frozen` now recomputes the canonical manifest digest, calls assignment validation, checks execution-source hashes and tokenizer identity, and compares private labels, public inputs, and config identity with the manifest. The mock gate binds to the frozen manifest and config. **Smallest correction:** retain these checks and the single paid directory on every resume. **Additional API need:** none.

### 15. E7 semantic controls and E8 internal use remain unobserved

**Severity: major for RQ2/RQ3 causal claims. Evidence:** non-annotated text can still answer the question, and lexical distinctness does not rule out paraphrases. Citation provenance does not establish entailment or use. **Current resolution:** E7 plans mark semantic non-support unverified and paid readiness false; no E7 paid result is claimed. E8 marks internal use, citation entailment, and counterfactual outcomes unobservable. **Smallest correction:** blind semantic review of frozen whole-sentence triples before E7 approval; preserve matched receiver state and document residual length differences. **Additional API need:** none for review; future paired E7 inference needs separate approval.

### 16. Annotated article separation does not establish necessary complementarity

**Severity: major for the central information-access mechanism. Evidence:** the reviewer inspected all eight frozen questions and supporting source documents before inspecting any paid answer or effect. Six contain strong answer cues within one support document: `5abace6b5542996cc5e49e99`, Arthur Quinlan sentence 1 names Che Guevara; `5a8080365542992097ad2fed`, Flexible-fuel vehicle sentence 2 explicitly associates hydrogen with bi-fuel vehicles; `5abfca465542993fe9a41e65`, the league article sentence 0 repeats the question's distinctive 121th-season descriptor and its title gives the answer; `5a7c651a55429935c91b5188`, Indigenous Australians sentence 4 supplies 80,000 years BP; `5ac540365542994611c8b470`, Loughborough University sentences 0–1 repeat the identifying university/date descriptors; `5abe4a365542993f32c2a0dc`, Chicco sentence 0 repeats the Artsana/50-years descriptors and supplies the country count. In the remaining two questions, each separated article independently supplies the shared answer attribute: vocalist for Danny Jones/Aleksi Sihvonen, and Spain for the national parks. Fully verifying a statement about both entities can still require both documents; answer-string availability is a weaker requirement than verification.

**Current resolution:** this source-only review identifies a construct-validity limitation, not measured single-document LLM accuracy. No question is removed, replaced, or reweighted, and the complete planned pilot must be retained. E6 still identifies the effect of relocating annotated articles under the stated protocol; it does not establish that the relocation removes answer information that is necessary to generate the scored answer string. **Smallest correction:** distinguish the placement estimand from an evidence-necessity estimand. Before a confirmatory mechanism study, preregister and blindly apply a source-level complementarity rubric to a fresh cohort, identifying direct-answer cues, cross-document joins, and alternative candidate evidence. Describe this selection as a new target population and keep the current pilot exploratory. **Additional API need:** none for the source review or claim correction; fresh confirmatory receiver responses require later approval. A positive E6 interaction alone would not repair this limitation.

### 17. Official answer scores are not semantic correctness judgments

**Severity: moderate for measurement, major if used as mechanism evidence. Evidence:** on `5ac3044f5542990b17b154dc`, all six broadcast revised responses across the two placements are `vocalists`, while the reference is `vocalist`; official token F1 and EM are both zero. `Ernesto "Che" Guevara` scores 80 F1 against `Che Guevara`; `more than 120` scores 85.714 against `more than 120 countries`. These behaviors are reproducible properties of the prespecified evaluator, not necessarily false factual beliefs. **Current resolution:** official F1 remains the unchanged primary endpoint. No outcome-dependent alias list, stemming rule, or semantic rescoring is substituted. **Smallest correction:** label incorrect/nonexact counts as metric-defined, show the raw examples, and distinguish string-score error from factual error. Any semantic endpoint for a future study needs a prespecified rubric applied consistently. **Additional API need:** none for this audit; no paid judge is needed to describe the observed surface-form cases.

**Status: COMPLETE, eight unique questions and all four cells per question.** The paid ledger records 144 successful completions and 144 HTTP attempts, with zero retries, rejections, or uncertain requests. Provider usage is complete: 92,594 input tokens and 34,564 output tokens. Recorded runtime is 289.448 seconds. Monetary cost remains unverified because the deployment tariff is not established. Six completions and 36 attempts remain within the authorization; this is not a reason to run more inference. E7 paid inference is NOT_RUN.

The four mean official answer-F1 scores, in points, are 35.714 co-located private revision, 79.762 co-located broadcast, 45.714 distributed private revision, and 81.548 distributed broadcast. The communication changes are +44.048 and +35.833 points, respectively. Each placement has four questions helped, none harmed, and four unchanged in final F1. These are within-pilot observations. The secondary exact sign-flip p-values are both .125 even though the exploratory bootstrap intervals exclude zero; the sparse eight-question sample and different reference constructions do not support selecting the more favorable inferential result.

The primary interaction is **−8.214 F1 points**, with exploratory 95% whole-question bootstrap interval **[−30.000, +5.357]** and exact two-sided sign-flip **p = 1.0**. Its point direction is opposite to the working hypothesis; its magnitude and direction are too unstable to establish a population effect. Six questions have exactly zero interaction; one contributes +14.286 points and one −80 points. Removing the six zero contrasts leaves four equally weighted sign states. This p-value is not evidence of equivalence.

The two nonzero interactions are especially sensitive to realized proposals and surface forms. In the Che Guevara question, co-located private revision retains one correct proposal and two abstentions, so majority selects `noanswer`; distributed private revision has three distinct normalized answers and the fixed tie rule selects `Ernesto "Che" Guevara` for 80 F1. Both broadcast conditions select `Che Guevara` for 100. In the Rosi Golan/Chicco question, broadcast selects `more than 120` in co-location and `more than 120 countries` under distribution. This is a 14.286-point string-score difference for the same substantive numerical answer. The frozen official scores and tie rule are retained. These traces do not justify describing the interaction as a clean change in multi-hop evidence utilization.

The placement manipulation itself is mechanically compliant: each question preserves the same six full source documents, with two per agent and the intended annotated-article ownership. NoComm makes all annotated support accessible to 8/24 co-located nodes and 0/24 distributed nodes. Broadcast delivers 48 directed peer messages per placement, totaling 96; 36 and 40 source-fact instances, respectively, are transmitted. Newly accessible annotated-support instances total 26 per placement. All annotated support becomes accessible to 12/24 co-located and 14/24 distributed revised nodes. These are repeated node/message events, not 48 independent questions. Twelve of the 26 broadcast nodes with all annotated support are nonexact under EM; objection 17 prevents interpreting every such event as semantic failure. Exact-candidate rejection occurs in two co-located and three distributed private-revision question-cells and in no broadcast cells. This is consistent with proposal adoption or agreement formation, while source-information utilization remains unidentified.

An additional raw-trace check is particularly informative: in **all 16 placement–question broadcast cells**, the selected final answer already appears among the initial normalized agent proposals, and its official F1 equals the highest initial-agent F1. Initial and revised any-agent exact coverage remain 4/8 in co-location and 5/8 under distribution. This is a supported descriptive finding that broadcast preserves/selects an available best-scoring proposal in this pilot. It is not an implemented oracle selector, does not prove that citing source facts caused the adoption, and does not establish that majority voting will continue to select the best candidate on a fresh sample. In these data, the large communication improvements concern proposal utilization and agreement rather than expansion of the set of exact answers produced by any agent.

## Final reviewer decision

**REVISE.** The implementation completed the authorized pilot with correct accounting and a valid narrow placement contrast. The central scientific mechanism remains unresolved: annotated-article separation did not establish necessary information removal, the two nonzero interactions depend strongly on proposal realization and answer forms, and close prior work already covers broad information-asymmetry and proposal/readout ideas. A larger replication of the same eight-question construct alone would not resolve these objections.

The smallest next correction requires no API calls: state the narrow estimand, preserve all pilot results, specify an independent source-complementarity rubric and target population, compare the intervention precisely with the verified prior work, and preregister a fresh cohort and measurement plan. Only then choose an appropriate confirmatory sample size and request a separate budget. This decision does not require a favorable pilot effect, and it is not a prediction of WWW acceptance. No confirmatory inference is authorized or executed by this review.
