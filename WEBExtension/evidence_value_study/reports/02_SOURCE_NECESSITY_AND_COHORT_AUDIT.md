Paid inference STOP.** The candidates are an outcome-blind annotation queue, not a validated source-necessity population or final evaluation cohort. ## Exposure audit and denominators

The audit starts from the immutable pre-implementation baseline of 140595 historical regular files. It scans 139382 historical text inputs totaling 1885157821 bytes, preserving exact path/hash/byte/encoding records in `source_validation/exposure_inputs.json`. UTF-16 and CP1252 legacy exports are decoded explicitly; zero selected text inputs remain unreadable. Five malformed legacy JSON exports retain ID-regex and direct question-literal fallback, and are identified in `exposure_audit.json`. Binary checkpoints/parquets, ignored environments, symlink targets outside this bounded baseline, inaccessible histories and external/pretraining exposure are not semantically audited. Raw full source catalogs are availability records, not experimental exposure, and are explicitly excluded from the exposure scan.

All historical text categories present in the baseline are included: E1–E8 artifact trees and corrected E3 variants; controller train, validation, pilot and held-out partitions and prior versions; prior selection/tuning files and request caches; local legacy evaluations and streams; `research_discovery_loop` reports/results/manifests; and external-validation original/amendment pilot, annotation, confirmation, sealed-label and ranking files. This is automated text membership inspection, not a claim that a human manually read every file. Each exclusion's exact evidence path is retained by ID and/or normalized-question match.

Identity exclusion closes over both exact dataset ID and normalized question text (`NFKC`, Unicode casefold, Unicode word-token join), including cross-ID aliases. Prior selected reservations are excluded whether or not inference was completed. The external-validation rank manifests contain all remaining pool IDs; their reserved 20 pilot, 24 annotation and first 1000 confirmation units are excluded. Ranking-only tail membership is separately recorded and never mislabeled as proof of model-outcome exposure.

HotpotQA has 7321 observed exposed/excluded IDs among 7405 source rows, leaving 84 rows without an observed match. Only 2074 source rows contain both distinct benchmark support-title documents plus at least four non-support original documents under the structural checks; only **10** of those survive the exposure exclusions. That is insufficient for 30 target plus 10 reserve. Benchmark support titles frequently are absent from the local fullwiki retrieval context, so support labels alone cannot establish usable source availability.

The strict Hotpot exclusion is dominated by two earlier population enumerations: `next_phase/planning_artifacts/fresh_pool.json` (6809 IDs) and `readout_reassessment/results/fresh_pool_verification.json` (6810 matched IDs including an edge reference). These are not evidence that every listed question received model inference. Ignoring only those two enumeration files would leave 1681 source-capable Hotpot questions without another observed match; this sensitivity is recorded in `exposure_population_sensitivity.json`. The prepared MuSiQue queue is an explicitly conditional alternative allowing ranking-only tail membership, not a claim that its freshness passes the stricter Hotpot policy. Both policies and their limitations must be reviewed before population approval.

MuSiQue has 1252 answerable two-hop source rows. The conservative structural screen requires exactly two marked supports, two decomposition steps, at least four original non-support documents, nonempty whole texts and unique source titles. It yields 604 source-capable rows, of which 351 survive observed prior selected/question-text exposures and normalized-question deduplication. Duplicate titles are conservatively rejected at the whole-row stage. Across the paired MuSiQue source IDs, 1054 have observed exclusion evidence. All 351 remaining structural candidates are ranking-only members; excluding all such membership gives zero. Availability of these 351 does not establish semantic source necessity or meet an unconditional freshness requirement.

## Frozen priority and construction

`frozen/private_candidates.json` contains 40 deterministic candidates in priority order: first 30 target priority and next 10 reserve priority. `frozen/candidate_manifest.json` omits gold answers, answer aliases and support assignments. Seed `E9-candidate-priority-v1-20261016` ranks eligible IDs by SHA256 before any new model outcome. This preparation calls no model or provider API and uses no existing model correctness, answer, disagreement or effect estimate as a selection feature.

Each global evidence union has exactly six original documents: two benchmark-designated supports and four original non-support documents. Four distractors are fixed by a question/index hash before inference, independently of model outcomes and without answer-conditioned document editing or replacement. The global union is then hash-shuffled; document IDs follow this shuffled union order and are not fixed support labels. Lexical diagnostics find zero prepared cases with the gold answer string in the question or distractor subset, and four cases with an answer string in some title. The latter remain flagged for genuine human inspection, not declared leaked or semantically invalid by a string match. These observations do not prove source sufficiency or necessity.

The candidate list is intentionally retained with unresolved qualifications. It must not be promoted to a 30-question qualified cohort because it reaches a numeric target. Use the fixed reserve order only after documented pre-inference human rejection. If fewer than 30 qualify, STOP and amend before inference; never replace candidates based on model outcomes.

## Required human review and present gate

The two blinded packages each contain 360 cases: nine separately randomized evidence subsets per candidate. Reviewer-facing records contain only opaque case ID, question and neutral source ID/title/text. They omit dataset/question IDs, gold, benchmark support flags, hidden subset name and study hypothesis. Case IDs differ by reviewer; source IDs and order differ by case. The separate private key maps each case to A-only, B-only, distractor-only, A+B, full union, full union minus A, full union minus B, or either exact planned missing-source receiver context. Repeated question text can reveal related variants if reviewers compare records; controlled one-case-at-a-time presentation and instructions reduce, but do not eliminate, this blinding limitation.

`annotations/reviewer_registry.json` has two unassigned human reviewer slots and an unassigned human adjudicator. `labels_R1.jsonl`, `labels_R2.jsonl` and `adjudication_template.jsonl` contain only empty judgment fields. They are not fabricated completed labels. The preserved preexisting annotation files in other study directories are not imported and do not qualify E9 questions.

Qualification requires **two independent qualified HUMAN source-necessity reviewers plus documented human adjudication** establishing: A-only insufficient; B-only insufficient; all four distractors insufficient; A+B sufficient; complete union sufficient; full union minus A insufficient; full union minus B insufficient; both exact planned missing-source receiver contexts insufficient; both support documents necessary; and no title/metadata leakage. Reviewers must supply evidence-linked reasons, answers or missing links, uncertainty and actual identities/timestamps. A model judgment, benchmark label, lexical match, null-filled template or unresolved disagreement cannot satisfy this gate. All reviews remain pending, so **qualified count = 0, completed qualified human reviewers = 0, paid gate = STOP**. The ranking-membership and source-authentication limitations are separate scientific gates even after human review.
