This is a record of the completed bounded investigation, not four fabricated rounds of independent experiments. The two rounds organize overlapping source checks into logical challenge/revision passes; they are not separately timestamped independent studies. All tests were offline. Candidate creation and ranking were held provisional until raw evidence and primary methods were checked.

## Round 1 — Activity-controlled routing as the leading attempt

Proposed K1, with K2/K4/K6 as distinct alternatives. Favoring evidence: E5 cost differences and recipient imbalance; W4’s controlled null is more relevant than unconstrained gain. Attacks: node and edge pruning are already separate in AGP/AgentDropout, causal subgraph execution in E2-Explainer, and organizational intervention in OpenMAS-GCom.

New contradictory evidence:17 primary references were assembled across the investigation; L1/L2 methods directly discuss execution attribution and actual token cost. Offline test: enumerate all 64 loop-free labeled graphs on three agents. Each two-edge/one-fixed-recipient class has size 1. P learned seeds 19/31 each choose one receiver for all 300 questions. Fixed recipient/edge count cannot support an edge-rewiring counterfactual for that dominant action. CW/CCW is a labeled assignment, not an unlabeled motif comparison.

Revision: an all-recipient matched update or N>=4 design can define another effect, but it changes the question/instrument and cannot rescue a novel general claim from the current result. Cost rises from simple replay to at least 48N generations for two source regimes/two backbones. K1/K2/K4/K6 do not pass final gates. The gap map was narrowed accordingly.

## Round 2 — Provenance dependence and source-conditioned readout

Proposed K3 as an outside-the-original-routing-narrative attempt; scrutinized K5, K7, K8 and K9 separately. Favoring evidence: readout sensitivity, minority proposal loss and potential upstream-source repetition are consequential.

New contradictory literature: L7 explicitly constructs controlled shared-source descendants and independent complementary evidence; L11 already varies supporting-document imbalance and misinformation; L8 fixes proposals while changing verification evidence; L10/L12 precede sufficiency/abstention. L14 includes real calibration failure, and L1 includes worker failure.

Offline tests: reaggregate frozen E5 without gold in rules; strict majority retains +7.667 pp communication gain, contradicting a universal-disappearance narrative. E6’s interaction is negative and uncertain. Source metadata do not certify derivation lineage or semantic necessity. MuSiQue has 2410 unique normalized questions and 2366 confirmation units after existing reservations. Arithmetic external traces share 500 question IDs and do not expose the semantic-abstention intervention. Power sensitivity at 5 pp requires 3803 questions for SD 1, beyond the qualified candidate pool even before source-label attrition.

Revision: a real-Web provenance design would require a new independently validated population; a sufficiency interaction would need both novelty and variance gates resolved. A small pilot would validate plumbing without deciding the science. K3/K5/K7/K8/K9 fail present gates. No new candidate was manufactured to fill the quota.
