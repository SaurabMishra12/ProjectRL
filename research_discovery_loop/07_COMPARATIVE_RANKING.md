# Comparative ranking

No candidate is ranked as a surviving paper direction. The ordering below is a retrospective record of investigation priority: K5, K1, K3, K4, K7, K2, K8, K6, K9. It is not a recommendation to fund the first item. Scores are investigator judgments on 0–5, not probabilities or a validated instrument. No average is used to defeat a hard rejection.

## K5 — Does source sufficiency determine how aggregation changes the measured value and harm of communication?

- **Scientific importance: 4/5.** A readout that raises answerable EM may also select unsupported answers.
- **Novelty: 2/5.** No substantive mechanism beyond combined existing questions established; current external traces are ineligible, labels pending and high-variance power exceeds the available pool.
- **Existing evidence: 4/5.** Frozen P gain changes from 8 pp to 1 pp under aware voting, but remains 7.667 pp under strict majority; external arithmetic has no semantic-abstention intervention.
- **Causal identifiability: 3/5.** A balanced interaction on semantically qualified paired Web contexts is unmeasured.
- **Feasibility: 2/5.** Qualified labels, matched private revisions, immutable backbones and variance small enough for the independent population.
- **Statistical strength: 2/5.** The existing rows do not supply a fresh, qualified question-level power estimate; report 09 gives explicit sensitivity bounds.
- **WWW relevance: 5/5.** A readout that raises answerable EM may also select unsupported answers. Web specificity must be demonstrated, not asserted from the title.
- **Robustness: 3/5.** A tight null would bound the interaction for those contexts; weak abstention prevalence makes a null uninformative.
- **Reproducibility: 4/5.** Raw references and code are preserved; provider and historical execution provenance remain bounded.

Decisive missing evidence: Qualified labels, matched private revisions, immutable backbones and variance small enough for the independent population.

Closest primary works: L8, L10, L12. Hard gate: **REJECT**. A strong findings paper is not justified at present.

## K1 — Do recipient activations, rather than edge routing, explain reported sparse-agent savings?

- **Scientific importance: 4/5.** Operators must know whether to optimize participation or communication.
- **Novelty: 1/5.** No substantive novelty established beyond existing component diagnosis; dominant N=3 in-star has no alternative graph conditional on its active recipient and edge count.
- **Existing evidence: 4/5.** E5 has 912 recipient-count mismatches; P seed 19/31 each activate one receiver on all 300 questions; W4 is inconclusive.

- **Feasibility: 2/5.** A multi-system effect distinct from known node pruning; nondegenerate randomization support and provider-level cost logs.
- **Statistical strength: 2/5.** The existing rows do not supply a fresh, qualified question-level power estimate; report 09 gives explicit sensitivity bounds.
- **WWW relevance: 4/5.** Operators must know whether to optimize participation or communication. Web specificity must be demonstrated, not asserted from the title.
- **Robustness: 3/5.** An adequately precise zero routing effect would bound value for the registered systems, not establish universal absence.
- **Reproducibility: 4/5.** Raw references and code are preserved; provider and historical execution provenance remain bounded.

Decisive missing evidence: A multi-system effect distinct from known node pruning; nondegenerate randomization support and provider-level cost logs.

## K3 — When does repeated upstream Web evidence become false independent corroboration during agent communication?

- **Existing evidence: 1/5.** Saved facts have titles and sentence IDs, but these do not certify upstream independence; repeated proposals in E8 are only a diagnostic.
- **Causal identifiability: 2/5.** A causal contrast on genuinely traced Web copying, rather than synthetic copies, would be more specific.
- **Feasibility: 1/5.** A licensed corpus with validated derivation lineages, matched independent alternatives and blinded factuality labels.
- **Statistical strength: 1/5.** The existing rows do not supply a fresh, qualified question-level power estimate; report 09 gives explicit sensitivity bounds.
- **WWW relevance: 5/5.** Syndication and copying can amplify a single erroneous source into apparent agreement. Web specificity must be demonstrated, not asserted from the title.
- **Robustness: 3/5.** A precise null would be informative only if lineage and intervention exposure were validated.
- **Reproducibility: 2/5.** Raw references and code are preserved; source lineage is unavailable.

Decisive missing evidence: A licensed corpus with validated derivation lineages, matched independent alternatives and blinded factuality labels.

Closest primary works: L7, L11, L10. Hard gate: **REJECT**. A strong findings paper is not justified at present.

- **Causal identifiability: 2/5.** A correctness-sensitive sequential guarantee under realistic dependence is absent, not supplied by ordinary SPRT thresholds.
- **Feasibility: 2/5.** Adequately many fresh late-correction cases and a calibrated stopping policy selected without test outcomes.

## Full-study comparisons for the three most scrutinized rejected ideas

K1 requires a nondegenerate graph/action intervention and multi-system value beyond existing node/edge diagnosis. A four-agent, two-source-regime, two-backbone matched revision comparison costs at least 48N successful generations before extra graph arms. At illustrative SD 0.5 and a 5 pp interaction, N≈951 gives 45,648 calls; power is not established by historical three-agent outcomes.

K3 requires independently validated upstream derivations before any scientific pilot count exists. A minimal two-lineage-setting, two-backbone design costs 24N calls (or 48N when crossed with metadata disclosure), excluding source construction. No qualified N or effect variance is available. Zero inference is presently useful.

K5 has the clearest existing instrumentation and strongest measured readout sensitivity, but weak substantive novelty and no eligible external mechanism replication. The conditional paired-context design uses 36N calls across two backbones. N≈951 at assumed SD 0.5 means 34,236 successes; at SD 1, N≈3803 exceeds the 2366-question pool. A20-question plumbing run cannot settle that feasibility problem. Details and adverse outcomes are preserved in 05 and 09.
