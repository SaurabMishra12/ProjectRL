# Final scientific recommendation

**C — NO JUSTIFIABLE WWW MAIN-TRACK RESEARCH QUESTION FOUND**

No research direction is endorsed for a new main-track submission or paid pilot on the present evidence. Nine distinct questions were evaluated, with zero final survivors after two substantive adversarial rounds. This conclusion is bounded to the investigated formulations and repository; it does not assert that multi-agent Web reasoning has no open problems.

The strongest measured result is a readout-dependent communication gain on 300 repeatedly inspected HotpotQA questions. It is scientifically useful as an audit, but proposal/readout separation, source-conditioned abstention, graph participation and provenance dependence all have close primary precedents. The remaining specific formulations lack a new mechanism, a validated manipulation, a suitable independent population or a feasible decisive study. Report 02 distinguishes these failures from a claim that every exact contrast is already settled.

Two adverse facts prevent a convenient narrative. Communication still improves strict-majority P exact match by 7.667 pp; abstention-aware voting is not a universal repair. E6’s 8-question placement interaction is -8.214 pp with CI [-30,+5.357], and none of its cases is independently necessity-qualified. The external arithmetic zero is induced by readout identity and does not replicate a semantic-abstention mechanism. Corrected E3-v2 is inconclusive. Current-code defects cannot explain historical checkpoint performance without provenance.

## Most productive alternative

Preserve this as a reproducible research audit and build a small **independently adjudicated source-trace resource** before selecting a new hypothesis. The immediate work is measurement: distinguish document identity from upstream derivation, annotated support from semantic sufficiency, answer correctness from source faithfulness, and real provider usage from transmitted text. The existing blinded templates can support this work, but pending templates are not completed annotation, and this is not a ready benchmark contribution. No additional model inference is recommended now.

Reconsider research only if that work reveals a reproducible, consequential failure whose prediction is distinct from the close literature and whose clean intervention has adequate independent data. A predefined positive or negative outcome must both change a Web-system design decision. The counterfactual designs in 05 show what would be needed and why they currently fail. There is no winning title, confirmed mechanism, approved pilot, guaranteed power or acceptance prediction to supply.

All reported new work is offline: zero paid Azure calls, zero paid judges, zero PPO training, zero model inference. Historical artifacts were preserved; see verification.json and artifact_manifest.json for the completed preservation check. The old six unused E6 completion slots remain unauthorized.
