# Reviewer red-team report

These are 36 separately articulated **simulated perspectives by one investigator**, not 36 reviewers or independent peer review. Delegated audits did not deliver completed usable reports and are not counted as independent validation. The investigator performed the material source and raw-output checks. The simulation supplies structured criticism but cannot certify novelty or publication readiness.

Severity: FATAL rejects the present formulation; MAJOR requires a new justified design; MODERATE is a reporting/robustness concern. No candidate survives the combined gate.

## K1 — Do recipient activations, rather than edge routing, explain reported sparse-agent savings?

Strongest argument in favor: Operators must know whether to optimize participation or communication.

Strongest rejection reason: L1/L2/L4/L5 already distinguish participation, edges and execution costs. No substantive novelty established beyond existing component diagnosis; dominant N=3 in-star has no alternative graph conditional on its active recipient and edge count.

Unresolved severity: FATAL.

Minimal evidence required: A mechanism-level distinction from L1, L2, L4, L5 supported by exact methods and a decisive new prediction.

Should survive now: **NO**. This perspective does not claim that a future reformulation is impossible.

### Reviewer B — Causal identification

Strongest rejection reason: The exact controlled direct effect of evidence routing at fixed revision schedule remains unmeasured here. A multi-system effect distinct from known node pruning; nondegenerate randomization support and provider-level cost logs.

Unresolved severity: MAJOR / FATAL under current support.

Minimal evidence required: Freeze all initial states, activate all receivers under both peer and own-state updates, randomize source assignments and match token ceilings; use N>=4 where in-star support needs rewiring.

Should survive now: **NO**. This perspective does not claim that a future reformulation is impossible.

Unresolved severity: MAJOR.
