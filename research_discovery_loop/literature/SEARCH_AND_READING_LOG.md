The review examines interventions, controls, data regimes, demonstrated outcomes and limitations. Author-reported outcomes are not treated as our independent replication. For papers without a separately headed limitations section, scope and limitations are taken from methods, discussion and appendix definitions and marked as investigator interpretation. Some preprints may also have conference versions; only verified editions are claimed, so absence of a venue label is not a claim that no acceptance exists.

D3MAS was screened but was not used to establish a novelty verdict. Other unresolved search leads remain background only. The literature-search-arxiv workflow informed the search. Browser primary-source reading was used; no custom bulk arXiv downloader or API loop was run in this phase. Check the [arXiv API terms](https://info.arxiv.org/help/api/index.html) and each paper’s license before redistribution. Paper texts were not copied into the repository.
